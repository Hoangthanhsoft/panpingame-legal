## 1. Summary

- There is no account and no sign-in. We never ask for a name, email address, phone number, date of birth, photo, contacts or location.
- There are no advertisements and no third-party analytics or advertising tools. We do not sell or share data.
- Your game progress stays on your device.
- The Game sends anonymous play events (for example, "level 3 won with 2 stars") so that we can see which levels are too hard. These events carry a random code created on your device. They never carry your name or IP address.

## 2. Data stored on your device

The Game saves the following in your browser's storage, or in the app's storage on a phone:

- levels completed and stars earned;
- sound and language settings;
- a random code (for example, `3f9a-07c2-b1e4`).

The random code is created on your device. It is not linked to you, to your device's advertising ID, or to any account. Clearing the website's data, or uninstalling the app, deletes all of this. We hold no copy and cannot restore it.

## 3. Anonymous play events

While you play, the Game sends small messages to `panpin.amarantineai.com/e.gif`. Each message contains only the following:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Event | game opened, level started, first move, heart lost, level ended, retry |
| Level and result | level 3; won with 2 stars, or lost |
| Seconds | time spent in the level |
| Obstacle number | the obstacle that cost a heart |
| Random code | the code described in section 2 |
| Game version and language | 1.1; English |

These events are stored in Cloudflare Workers Analytics Engine. **We do not store an IP address, browser details or any other identifier with them.** They are kept for about three months and are then deleted automatically.

We use these events only to keep the Game working and to balance its difficulty, that is, to support the internal operation of the Game. We do not use them to contact anyone, to build profiles or for advertising, and we do not combine them with any other data.

## 4. Delivering and protecting the Game

The Game is hosted by **Cloudflare, Inc.**, 101 Townsend Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, United States.

To deliver the pages over HTTPS and to protect them from attacks, Cloudflare processes the IP address of each connection and ordinary request data. Cloudflare also uses the country of the connection to refuse access from Vietnam, where the Game is not offered. We do not keep these connection logs. Cloudflare describes its own handling of data at https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/.

The Game itself sets no cookies. Cloudflare may set a short-lived security cookie to filter automated traffic. No cookie is used for advertising.

## 5. Children and parents

Panpin is made for children aged 6 and older and for the adults who play with them.

- We do not collect personal information from children, and playing never requires giving any.
- Links that leave the Game (this policy, the Terms of Use and the support page) are placed behind a **parent gate**: a question that an adult can answer.
- After about 30 minutes of continuous play, the Game shows a gentle reminder to take a break.
- There is nothing to buy in this version.

If you believe that a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 6. If you email or call us

We receive what you send: your email address or phone number, and your message. We use it only to reply. We delete it within 90 days after the matter is closed, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Messages that appear to come from a child are deleted without being used. A parent or guardian should contact us instead.

## 7. Google Play app

If you install Panpin from Google Play, the app works in the same way as the website. It uses the same on-device storage (section 2) and sends the same anonymous play events (section 3). The app requests no special permissions other than internet access. Google handles downloads and updates under Google's own privacy policy.

## 8. Legal grounds (EEA and United Kingdom)

Where the GDPR or the UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

- our legitimate interest in delivering a working, safe and fairly balanced game (sections 3 and 4); and
- the need to answer your request (section 6).

You may object to processing that is based on legitimate interest.

## 9. Your rights

You may ask us what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. You may also complain to the data protection authority in the country where you live. We will reply within one month.

Because we have no accounts and store no names or IP addresses, we usually cannot link play events to a person. Clearing the Game's data on your device removes your random code. Events already sent are deleted automatically after about three months.

## 10. Processing outside your country

Cloudflare's network is global. Your data may therefore be processed outside your country, including in the United States.

## 11. Security

All pages and play events are sent over HTTPS. Only the people who answer support messages can read them.

## 12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. If a change is important, for example if purchases are added, we will show a notice in the Game before the change applies.

## 2. Dữ liệu lưu trên thiết bị

Trò chơi lưu trong bộ nhớ của trình duyệt, hoặc bộ nhớ của ứng dụng trên điện thoại:

- các màn đã hoàn thành và số sao đạt được;
- cài đặt âm thanh và ngôn ngữ;
- một mã ngẫu nhiên (ví dụ: `3f9a-07c2-b1e4`).

Mã ngẫu nhiên được tạo trên thiết bị, không gắn với bạn, với mã quảng cáo của thiết bị hay với tài khoản nào. Xóa dữ liệu trang web hoặc gỡ ứng dụng sẽ xóa toàn bộ. Chúng tôi không giữ bản sao và không thể khôi phục.

Trong lúc chơi, Trò chơi gửi các tin nhắn nhỏ tới `panpin.amarantineai.com/e.gif`. Mỗi tin chỉ gồm:

| Trường | Ví dụ |

| Màn và kết quả | màn 3; thắng 2 sao hoặc thua |
| Số giây | thời gian chơi trong màn |

Chúng tôi chỉ dùng các sự kiện này để giữ Trò chơi hoạt động tốt và cân chỉnh độ khó, tức là phục vụ vận hành nội bộ của Trò chơi. Chúng tôi không dùng chúng để liên hệ với ai, lập hồ sơ hay quảng cáo, và không kết hợp với dữ liệu nào khác.
